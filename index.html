    Welcome

    It’s lovely you’re here.

    If you’ve followed my journey before, welcome back.

    If we’ve never met before, I’m so pleased you found me.

    A few years ago, life took me in a direction I never expected.

    Kidney failure.
    Six years of dialysis.
    A transplant.
    Recovery.

    I quietly disappeared from the work I loved.

    Not because I wanted to…

    Because I simply didn’t have anything left in the tank.

    The good news…

    I’m back.

    Well…

    Sort of. 😄

    Because trying to become the old Jo-Dee took far too much energy… lol.

    So instead…

    I became Jo-Dee 2.0.

    A little older.
    Definitely wiser.
    Very grateful.

    And more passionate than ever about helping people navigate life’s challenges with hope, science and a little more laughter.

    Why I Do This

    During my recovery I spent several years volunteering at Lighthouse.

    Helping people facing loneliness.
    Homelessness.
    Debt.
    Relationship breakdown.
    Mental health challenges.

    Some had once led businesses or enjoyed highly successful careers. Others had travelled very different paths. Life had simply taken them somewhere they never expected.

    Life reminded me something very important.

    Everyone is carrying something.

    Sometimes people simply need someone who understands…

    Someone who listens…

    Someone who helps them take the next small step.

    That became the foundation of everything I now create.

    Why Organisations Work With Me

    Today’s workplaces don’t need more motivational speeches.

    They need practical tools that help people think more clearly, communicate more effectively and stay resilient when life gets messy.

    My sessions combine neuroscience, humour and real-life experience to create lasting change, not just a good hour.

    Whether your organisation is navigating change, supporting wellbeing, preventing burnout or developing stronger leaders, I help people leave with practical strategies they can use immediately.

    Because resilient people build resilient organisations.
